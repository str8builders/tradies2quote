## What changed
- **Sign-up path:** cookie notice is a slim bottom bar that waits for a scroll on "/"; hero fits a 1366×768 laptop; phone header says "Start free"; motion toggle moved into the footer; zoom allowed on "/".
- **Background:** `LiveWallpaper` draws a precomputed SVG terrain animated with compositor-only CSS (`src/app/_components/wallpaper/*`). No three.js on public pages.
- **Video:** `src/remotion/` recreates the app's screens with one fictional job (Sam Taylor, timber deck, $4,830.00). `npm run render:marketing` renders `public/videos/*` (demo wide/tall, hero loop), posters, six feature stills, and share files to git-ignored `marketing-media/` (15 s social cut, 60 s full tour). `MarketingVideo` plays files, poster-first, after page load, never under reduced motion or the motion toggle.
- **Layout:** hero → demo (chapters) → six feature cards → T2QCAL (with try-it calculator) → founder → pricing → FAQ → final CTA. HowItWorks, QuoteWorkflow and ten dead components removed (incl. fake LiveTicker/StatStrip stats).
- **Weight:** Sentry browser SDK loads only when `NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN` is set (production has none); Plus Jakarta Sans is one variable file; Fraunces and Inter are no longer preloaded; logos are a 15 KB WebP.
- **Search/accessibility:** FAQPage JSON-LD, `/t2qcal` and `/install` in the sitemap, contrast and label fixes.
