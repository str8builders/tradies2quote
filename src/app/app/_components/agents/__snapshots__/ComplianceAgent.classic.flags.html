  • Quote contains language that may sound like a guarantee (1 match).

    Soften absolute claims like 'guarantee', 'certified', 'warranty' to 'workmanship is to NZ Building Code'.

  • No exclusions listed — clients often query what is NOT covered.

    Copy 1–2 standard exclusions from the suggestions below.

  • No site assumptions noted (access, weather, substrate, power).

    A short list of assumptions protects you when conditions change.

  • Terms don't mention variations. Scope changes mid-job are common.

    Add the standard variations clause from the suggestions below.

// suggested clauses

  • Consents and council fees

    // exclusion

    Excludes building consent, resource consent, council inspection fees, and any associated levies — these are arranged and paid directly by the client unless specifically agreed in writing.

  • Hidden defects

    // exclusion

    Excludes remediation of hidden defects discovered once existing work is opened up (e.g. rot, borer, asbestos, faulty wiring, water damage). Any additional work required will be quoted separately before proceeding.

  • Asbestos and hazardous materials

    // exclusion

    Excludes identification, testing, removal, or disposal of asbestos or other hazardous materials. If found, work pauses and the client engages a licensed removalist at their cost.

  • Painting and decoration

    // exclusion

    Excludes painting, wallpapering, and decorative finishes unless explicitly itemised in the quote.

  • Electrical and plumbing

    // exclusion

    Excludes any electrical or plumbing work requiring a registered tradesperson unless explicitly itemised. These will be quoted by the appropriate licensed subcontractor.

  • Site access

    // assumption

    Assumes clear, safe access to the work area during agreed hours. Delays caused by restricted access (e.g. tenants, pets, deliveries) may incur additional charges at the agreed labour rate.

  • Weather delays

    // assumption

    Assumes acceptable working weather. Heavy rain, high winds, or extreme heat may delay outdoor work; rescheduled days are not chargeable but project completion may shift accordingly.

  • Power and water

    // assumption

    Assumes the client provides usable power (240 V AC) and water at the site for the duration of the work.

  • Existing substrate condition

    // assumption

    Assumes existing framing, lining, and substrates are sound. Any remediation of underlying defects is excluded (see exclusions).

  • Quote validity

    // term

    This quote is valid for 30 days from the issue date. After that, materials prices may have moved and the quote may need to be re-issued.

  • Variations

    // term

    Any change in scope must be agreed in writing before the change is carried out. Variations will be quoted separately and added to the final invoice.

  • Deposit and progress payments

    // term

    50% deposit on acceptance for jobs over $5,000. Progress payments for jobs over 2 weeks. Final payment due on practical completion.

  • Late payment

    // term

    Accounts unpaid 14 days after invoice may attract a late-payment fee of 2% per month. Materials remain the contractor's property until the invoice is paid in full.

// not legal advice — these are common clauses, adjust to your job
